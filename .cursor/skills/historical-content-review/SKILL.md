# Historical Content Review

This workflow requires current external research when factual claims are being established.

## Review

1. List each material historical claim.
2. Classify it under `docs/HISTORICAL_FANTASY_POLICY.md`.
3. Find and record strong sources.
4. Note disputes, chronology, geographic uncertainty, and anachronisms.
5. Separate polity, culture, language, religion, and legal status.
6. Identify gameplay abstractions.
7. Identify fantasy divergence and ensure it is not phrased as fact.
8. Review names and terminology.
9. Check source/data licenses.
10. Add source records and confidence.
11. Produce a release block list for unsupported central claims.

Do not use unsourced AI text as evidence.
